Halt the executions in Iran — Canada must act

As your constituent, I am writing with deep alarm at the Islamic Republic of Iran's execution of young protesters detained for demonstrating against the regime. These are political killings intended to silence dissent, and they violate Iran's obligations under international law.

I urge you to press the Government of Canada to:

1. Publicly and unequivocally condemn the executions and demand an immediate moratorium on the death penalty for protesters;
2. Impose Magnitsky and other targeted sanctions on the officials, judges, and prison authorities responsible;
3. Raise these cases directly at the UN and coordinate with allies to hold the regime accountable.

Please tell me what actions you and the government will take. Silence is not an option when lives are being taken for the act of protest.

Thank you.
